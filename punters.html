The gap nobody was filling

The racing system
fails punters.
We fix that.

Every year in South African racing, the NHA hands down fines and suspensions — for interference, for prohibited substance violations, for riding offences. Many of these rulings come after the official result stands, after the dividends have been paid out, and after the affected horses' races are long over.

The jockey or trainer pays their fine. The racing industry moves on. The punter who backed the affected horse, or who lost their wager because of that interference, receives nothing. No recourse. No acknowledgement. No credit.

P.A.S.A.'s Punter Justice Fund changes that.

The process

Four steps from
ruling to refund

01
📋
You register
Complete the free registration below. Your name, email, and primary bookmaker are confirmed and you're added to the P.A.S.A. distribution network.
02
⚖️
NHA rules on a race
When the NHA hands down a fine or suspension for actions that affected an official result, P.A.S.A. monitors the ruling and flags qualifying events.
03
🔍
P.A.S.A. audits
Our team cross-references the ruling with registered punter bets on the affected race. Eligible participants are identified automatically — no claims process required.
04
💳
Credits distributed
Betting wallet credits are distributed to your registered bookmaker account within 48 hours of the audit. You receive an email notification with full details.

Any NHA disciplinary ruling or fine that relates to actions which materially affected the outcome of a race — including riding interference, prohibited substance violations, and equipment infringements that impacted race results. P.A.S.A. reviews each ruling against a defined eligibility criteria before any distribution occurs. Rulings that relate to administrative or non-race-outcome matters do not qualify.
For the pilot programme, credit distributions are targeted at registered P.A.S.A. punters who held active accounts with supported bookmakers at the time of the affected race. Full bet-level verification with individual bookmakers is part of our Tier 2 expansion. In the pilot, all registered punters receive awareness notifications; credit eligibility is determined by bookmaker account verification.
Credit amounts vary per qualifying event and are determined by the severity of the ruling, the fund balance at the time, and the number of registered punters on affected races. P.A.S.A. publishes a full governance document outlining the distribution formula. We do not guarantee a specific rand value per event, but all distributions are funded directly from programme revenue — including Winner's Spotlight — before any P.A.S.A. fees are taken.
